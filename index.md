# Adaptive Learning

### Personalized AI-powered education

**Adaptive Learning** is a Windows desktop educational application currently under private development.

Our goal is to provide a personalized, interactive learning experience powered by artificial intelligence.

The application is designed to help learners understand concepts, ask questions, receive explanations, and progress through structured educational content at their own pace.

### Key objectives

- Adaptive AI tutoring and interactive explanations.
- Structured subjects, lessons, and learning objectives.
- Educational progress tracking and continuity.
- Local-first data management and privacy controls.
- Support for interchangeable AI providers.

### Current development status

Adaptive Learning is an early-stage, privately developed project intended initially for family use by adult learners.

The application is not currently available for public download or commercial purchase.

We are evaluating supported AI integrations, including Sign in with ChatGPT, to improve the quality and accessibility of personalized tutoring.

Future commercial development remains under consideration.
